## Full Description
SafeWalk is your essential companion for urban safety. 
Our community-driven app empowers you to report and view potential hazards in your city in real-time, helping everyone walk with confidence.

**Key Features:**
*   **Interactive Safety Map**: View incidents reported by the community on a live map (poor lighting, icy roads, suspicious activity, etc.).
*   **Real-time Filters**: Filter reports by type, severity, or date to see what matters most to you.
*   **Instant Reporting**: Witnessing a hazard? Report it in seconds with a photo, description, and automatic geolocation.
*   **Proximity Alerts**: Receive instant notifications when you are approaching a reported danger zone.
*   **Community Verification**: Vote on reports to confirm their validity and help the community stay informed.

**Why SafeWalk?**
Because safety is a collective effort. Join the SafeWalk community today and contribute to making your streets safer for everyone.
